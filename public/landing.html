Built with care

A coach, not a doctor, and the data stays theirs

The same safeguards in every version.

Not a doctor

It doesn't diagnose or change medication. For medical questions it points people to their doctor, and in an emergency to their local emergency number.

Their data, their call

People can see what the coach has saved, correct it, download it or delete their account at any time. Photos and PDFs aren't kept after it reads them.

Adults only

For people aged 18 and over.

Early test version
